# WoltFetch

## Register

product

## Product Purpose

An internal Hebrew RTL catalog for a retail merchant on Wolt. Staff pull live listings from Wolt, search by name or SKU, and attach a photo of the physical packaged product. Packaging photos stay in this app's database and are never sent back to Wolt.

## Users

Warehouse and store staff at a packing bench during business hours. They look up an item, compare the Wolt listing photo with the box in front of them, and upload or replace the packaging shot.

## Brand Personality

Warm, practical, warehouse-adjacent. Kraft paper, packing tape, and clear type. No delivery-app chrome, no dashboard theater.

## Anti-references

- Wolt teal consumer UI
- Generic SaaS cream dashboards
- Dark ops consoles
- Centered modals for item work

## Strategic Principles

- Search is the primary action, not browsing 3,000 cards.
- Wolt photos and packaging photos are visually equal and never mixed.
- Credentials and Wolt writes never leave the server. This app is read-only toward Wolt.
- Hebrew RTL is the interface, not a translation layer.
